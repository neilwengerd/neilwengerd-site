Each of us carries an ember, the spark of who we are. When it dims, you can do everything right and still feel stuck, exhausted, and like you’re performing. Campfire is where we sit together, tend the fire, and find the way back to ourselves.

Who Campfire is for

You’ve always had a fire to create, build, or teach. You made a career and found some success. You’re great at your work, but no longer sure you like it. You can’t tell whether there’s another way or only a different version of the same life. It's all too much, yet somehow not enough.

  • You rewrite the same email five times, and a project isn’t done until it’s flawless, even when no one else will notice.
  • You attach goals, schedules, and scorekeeping to things you used to do for fun.
  • You finally have a free afternoon and spend it explaining to yourself why you’re allowed to take it.
  • You feel okay about yourself on days you produce something, and not on days you don’t.
  • You’re surrounded by people who love and respect you, but none of them have seen what’s underneath the public version of you.
  • You scroll for a few minutes and your mood shifts depending on how someone else’s year is going.
  • You already know what you want, and you’re waiting for someone to say it’s okay to want it.

What Campfire offers

Over the past 15+ years, I've gotten the degree. Won the awards. Created a career. Built a business. Then I had to find out who I was without it. What I found is that my fire didn't go out. I just had to uncover the ember that was still there and give it space to burn again.

I won’t hand you five steps or an optimization plan. What I do isn’t about becoming someone new. It’s about clearing away what isn’t essential: the old unhelpful stories and scripts, the roles nobody asked you to take on, the habits that formed by default, and the noise that's been crowding out your ember. You’ll walk away less stuck, more hopeful, more alive, and lighter than you came. Who you’ve always been is still there. I help you find your way back.

My work is rooted in four truths that have helped me handle my fire in new ways.

  1. You are not alone.
  2. You are naturally creative, resourceful, and whole.
  3. You can be “ordinary” and still have significance.
  4. And you do not have to perform to be you.

The core of Campfire

We each arrive in a different place. No matter where you are, the work centers on four areas. Together, they fan the flame back to life.

Your Ember

We fall in love with what we do and follow it. But sometimes what we love stops working for us. I'll help you reconnect with what lights you up and separate who you are from what you do. You leave with a clear description of your ember in your own words.

The Two Halves of Life

We start life with the goal of becoming somebody. We push, we strive. But at a certain point, the striving wears us down. I’ll help you see what drove the first half of life and what’s asking to guide the second. You leave with a list of clear values to keep you grounded.

Time and Rest

We pour ourselves into our work. Sometimes that's what the season of life calls for. But without proper rhythms, our fire sputters. I’ll help you work out which season you’re actually in. You leave with a rhythm for your years, months, and weeks, with rest built in.

Defining Enough

A big part of tending our fire is knowing how much wood we actually need. Our culture insists it’s always “more.” But the truth is, fire burns best when it’s given just enough. I’ll help you name how much is enough. You leave with your own definition of it.
